# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Industries / Property Types
- Luxury apartment complexes
- Government run apartment complexes
- Distribution warehouses
- Offices of all kinds (law offices, tech offices, dental clinics, physiotherapy centers, corporate headquarters, research facilities)
- Kitchen cleaning for restaurants
- Wineries
- Stores
- Motels

## Services Offered
- Standard janitorial / cleaning
- Turnover cleaning (apartment move outs and move ins)
- Carpet cleaning
- Pressure washing
- High dusting

## Geographic Area
Alameda, Oakland, Hayward, San Leandro, Fremont, San Jose, San Francisco, San Mateo, Union City, Newark, Milpitas, Santa Clara, Richmond, Emeryville, Berkeley, Walnut Creek, Pleasanton, Dublin, Livermore, San Rafael, Vallejo, Concord, Daly City, Redwood City, Palo Alto, Mountain View, Sunnyvale, Vacaville

## Size Thresholds
- Apartments: 100+ units
- Offices: 7,000+ sq ft

## Target Decision Makers
- Property manager
- Facilities manager
- Procurement manager (for large apartment complexes)
- General manager (for stores and motels)

## Buying Signals
- New building recently opened
- Unhappy with current cleaning vendor
- High volume of tenant moves (turnover opportunity)
- Expansion or renovation underway

## Exclusions
- Small offices
- Existing customers
- Very large companies with long procurement cycles (e.g. Google, Apple) — not a priority target, but a welcome win if it happens

## Deal Size
- One time jobs: around $1,000
- Recurring contracts: $1,500+ per month

## Lead List Data Points
- Company name
- Address
- Contact name
- Title
- Email
- Phone
- Any relevant buying signal
